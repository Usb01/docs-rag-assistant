# Falcon Freight – Employee Handbook

Version 3.2. Applies to all full-time staff in the Dammam, Jubail and Riyadh offices.

## 1. Working hours

Office hours are Sunday to Thursday, 8:00 to 17:00, with a one-hour lunch break. Warehouse teams work in shifts published every second Thursday by the shift supervisor.

During Ramadan, office hours are reduced to 9:00 – 15:00. Warehouse shift lengths are reduced by two hours.

Overtime must be approved in advance by your line manager in the HR portal. Approved overtime is paid at 1.5× the hourly rate. On official public holidays, it's paid at 2×.

## 2. Annual leave

Employees receive 21 working days of paid annual leave per year. After five years of continuous service, this increases to 30 days.

Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 14 days before the first day of leave. Requests for more than 10 consecutive days need approval from both your line manager and the department head.

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are lost. Unused leave cannot be exchanged for cash, except when you leave the company.

## 3. Sick leave

Tell your line manager before 9:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is needed for any absence longer than one day.

Sick leave is paid in full for the first 30 days in a year, at 75% for the next 60 days, and unpaid after that.

## 4. Remote work

Office staff can work remotely up to two days per week once they've passed probation (90 days). Remote days must be agreed with your line manager and recorded in the HR portal by Wednesday for the following week.

Warehouse, fleet and customer-counter roles are not eligible for remote work.

When working remotely, you must use the company VPN and be reachable on Teams during office hours.

## 5. Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed through the Expenses module of the HR portal within 30 days of the expense. Attach an itemised receipt for every item over SAR 50.

Daily meal allowance on business trips is SAR 150 within Saudi Arabia and USD 75 abroad. Hotel bookings must go through the travel desk (travel@falconfreight-demo.com). Self-booked hotels are reimbursed only up to the travel-desk rate.

Mileage for using your own car is reimbursed at SAR 0.75 per km.

## 6. Training

Every employee has a training budget of SAR 4,000 per year. Courses over SAR 2,000 need department head approval. If you leave within 12 months of finishing a course costing more than SAR 5,000, the cost is deducted from your final settlement on a pro-rata basis.

## 7. Code of conduct

Gifts from customers or suppliers worth more than SAR 300 must be declared to Compliance within 5 working days. Cash gifts must never be accepted.

Conflicts of interest – for example, a relative working for a supplier – must be reported to HR.
