# Falcon Freight – Customer FAQ

## Tracking a shipment

You can track any shipment at track.falconfreight-demo.com using the 12-digit waybill number on your receipt. Tracking updates every 30 minutes while the shipment is moving.

If tracking hasn't changed for more than 48 hours, contact support with your waybill number and we'll open an investigation.

## Delivery times

- **Express (same city):** same day if booked before 12:00, otherwise next business day.
- **Standard domestic:** 2–4 business days between major cities (Dammam, Riyadh, Jeddah). Remote areas take up to 7 business days.
- **GCC road freight:** 4–6 business days to UAE, Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar and Oman, plus customs clearance.
- **International air:** 3–7 business days depending on destination.

Business days are Sunday to Thursday. Saturday deliveries are available in Dammam, Khobar and Riyadh for an extra SAR 25.

## Customs and duties

For international shipments, import duties and VAT are paid by the receiver unless the shipper chooses DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) when booking.

The commercial invoice must include HS codes, item values and country of origin. Missing documents are the most common cause of customs delays. Shipments held by customs for more than 10 days are returned to the sender at the sender's cost.

## Prohibited items

We don't carry lithium batteries shipped on their own, flammable liquids, perfumes over 100 ml, cash, firearms or perishable food without our cold-chain service. Shipments containing prohibited items are held and the shipper is charged a handling fee of SAR 200.

## Damaged or lost shipments

Report damage within 3 days of delivery, with photos of the package and contents. Lost shipments can be claimed 15 days after the expected delivery date.

Standard liability is limited to SAR 100 per kg, up to a maximum of SAR 2,500 per shipment. For higher-value items, buy shipment insurance at booking: 1.5% of the declared value, minimum SAR 30.

Approved claims are paid within 21 days to the original payment method.

## Changing or cancelling a booking

You can change the delivery address free of charge before the shipment is picked up. After pickup, an address change within the same city costs SAR 20. A change to another city is treated as a new shipment.

Bookings cancelled before pickup are refunded in full. Bookings cancelled after pickup are not refunded.

## Business accounts

Companies sending more than 50 shipments a month can open a business account with monthly invoicing, volume discounts and an account manager. Apply at falconfreight-demo.com/business. Accounts are usually approved within 3 business days.
